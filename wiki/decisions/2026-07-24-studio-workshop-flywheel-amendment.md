# Round 3: studio-workshop flywheel — an OPERATE amendment, not a re-decide

**Decision.** Sebastian's 2026-07-24 proposal (studio workshops as "the one thing",
2Key on ice) is **accepted as content, rejected as framing**: unanimously, all three
seats rule this is **not a third portfolio re-decision but an amendment to R2's
OPERATE layer** — the already-decided workshop line gets productized into the
**studio-workshop flywheel** (client pays the day, cameras run; one production unit
yields workshop fee + sponsor integration + a 30–40-min YouTube cut as proof +
long-form as email-gated list-builder). 2Key remains the only BUILD; "on ice" is
explicitly rejected — instead the R2 90-day pre-order test gets its start date (memo
signing) and 2Key becomes the **first "sponsor" inside every workshop** (KPI:
activated 2Key accounts). Precondition for everything: **R1/R2 + this amendment get
SIGNED by both founders** — three re-decisions in four days happened precisely
because the memos were never signed. Confidence: high on the frame (unanimous,
convergent across independent takes AND cross-examination); medium on price points
until the tripwire data exists. Status `proposed`: signature by both founders is the
activation, and this time Florian reportedly co-authored the direction (still
unverified in-session).

**Context.** Sebastian's long dictated statement (2026-07-24, persisted verbatim in
the second-brain vault as `raw/2026-07-24-narration-strategiewende-studio-workshops.md`):
15-month revenue history (YouTube+sponsoring, Skool premium 300→175 and internally
dead, workshops €2,500/day, failed Mitarbeiterschulung 12×€7,500, draining agency
work, early-2026 liquidity crisis, layoffs to two founders), Hostinger €2,500/video
with NO contract, n8n→Claude-Code content shift, comparison pain vs. Julian Ivanov
(~€50k/mo). New facts since R2: (1) first studio-workshop test with Pierre (GF,
Florian's acquaintance) recorded 2026-07-23 — technically worked, near-zero prep,
genuine founder energy, two hands-on sponsor integrations (Hostinger VPS, Data4Zero)
of near-dedicated quality; (2) both founders thought this through together; (3) the
R1/R2 memos were never signed. Sebastian asked for validation, extraction of the open
challenges, and answers.

**Reasoning.**
- *Meta (unanimous):* No written R1/R2 assumption has been falsified — Pierre is one
  warm, unpaid data point ("a dopamine hit, not data" — Hormozi; "one data point is
  not validation, novelty addiction is the creative's disease" — Chris Do via Blair
  Enns; "validate before you build; my two biggest mistakes were small TAM and lack
  of focus" — Neil). BUT an unsigned memo is a draft, not a decision (all three).
  Therefore: sign first, amend in the same act, then "re-read, never re-decide"
  applies with teeth. The studio format is a **repackaging of the existing workshop
  OPERATE line** (Hormozi), a **format iteration within the 80/20 R&D allowance**
  (Chris Do), the **productized form of an existing revenue line** (Neil) — it does
  not touch the BUILD commitment.
- *Business shape (unanimous):* As a cash machine the flywheel is strong — literally
  Hormozi's "capture, don't create" model; proof-over-promise ("clients choose the
  least risky option; the 30–40-min cut IS the recipe" — Chris Do); the one-sentence
  pitch passes the fifth-grader test. As an ASSET it is weak: EV = customers × LTGP ÷
  risk, three key-man risks, sponsorship revenue ≈ zero exit value (Hormozi);
  founder-bound revenue is a treadmill (Neil: NP-Digital rename, walked away from a
  $600k/mo course business). **Income play feeding the asset play**: the exit story
  remains 2Key; workshop/sponsor cash funds the season. Chris Do's partial dissent
  preserved: the 8–12h recordings are productizable "build once, sell many times"
  assets (his $1M-from-one-recorded-course precedent) — not exit assets, but more
  than a day-rate treadmill.
- *Price list (converged after cross-examination; Hormozi and Neil formally
  retracted their discount framing in favor of Chris Do's):* three options,
  top price first, value-add wording, NO discount vocabulary (consistent with the
  binding wording rule in the company-website wiki):
  1. **Vor-Ort beim Kunden** — ~2–2.5× base (~€12k+), unadvertised, on request only
     (top anchor; "the client compares you against yourself").
  2. **Studio privat** — no cameras, full confidentiality: ~1.5× (~€7.5k; Hormozi
     opened at €8.5k/+70%, Chris Do and Neil at +50% — founders fix the number).
     Privacy is the premium feature — this converts German camera-shyness from an
     objection into a price tier.
  3. **Studio Creator-Session (on camera)** — base **~€5,000** (the R2-mandated ~2×
     reprice of the old €2,500 day rate), *advertised standard and anchor*;
     "inklusive professionell produzierter Workshop-Film + Rohmaterial als Asset für
     Ihr Marketing". The economic effect of the camera "discount" survives, but the
     paper shows value addition, never justification ("re-design, don't discount";
     "never justify your price" — Chris Do). The damaging admission ("cameras run,
     that's why it costs less") is allowed in the sales CONVERSATION, not on the list.
- *Offer surface (unanimous):* externally ONE thing — only the studio workshop is
  advertised ("specialize externally, generalize internally"). On-site and private
  exist as unadvertised menu tiers. Masterclass courses: launch as decided
  (Skool-exit record), sell as **backend via the email sequence, not a parallel
  storefront** (Neil: one front door, several monetization layers; Hormozi: cash it,
  don't develop it).
- *Long-form monetization (unanimous, 3:0 against Sebastian's premium-paywall
  idea):* the 8–12h recording goes behind an **email gate as list-builder**, not a
  paywall — "the more you give, the richer you get"; "Get Flow. Monetize Flow. Then
  Add Friction." (Hormozi); "every ask resets the karmic account" (Chris Do); Neil:
  no track record for YouTube-Premium paywalls, the list is the ATM; recordings may
  become a paid bundle in 6–12 months INSIDE the email sequence.
- *YouTube mechanics (Neil, uncontested):* workshop cuts are **search assets, not
  browse content** — keyword-titled cuts ("Hostinger VPS für Claude Code aufsetzen —
  komplettes Setup mit echtem GF"), chapters, spoken keyword density; they start in
  the 20% slot of the R2 70/20/10 mix and earn their way into the 70% via
  **revenue per 1,000 views** (tracked per video: email opt-ins/1k views, booked
  first calls via per-video landing pages/UTM, sponsor conversions via unique codes).
  2,000 qualified > 20,000 generic views is affirmed by all three — but only as a
  measured claim, benchmarked monthly against their own numbers, not Julian Ivanov.
- *Sponsor thesis (Neil, uncontested):* hands-on in-workshop integration is the most
  aligned ad format (Mint-Mobile logic) — plausible premium between integration and
  dedicated pricing, but sell it with data (unique links, 4–6-video comparison vs.
  past overlays). **Blocking precondition: get the Hostinger quarterly commitment IN
  WRITING before building the content model around sponsor integrations** — 50–60%
  of revenue from one sponsor on a handshake is a cluster risk, not a relationship
  (already flagged in R2, doubly binding now). Second sponsor: Data4Zero is already
  in the room.
- *Funnel (Neil, uncontested):* email capture on every video; **quiz instead of lead
  form** (his +280% precedent) doubling as qualification (GF? team size? implements
  personally? camera OK?); landing page where the 30–40-min cut IS the proof;
  automated sequence to a first call. No cold ads at a €5k ticket; pipeline for the
  first bookings = 10k free Skool + existing list (+ outbound, below).
- *Avatar (converged):* "young tech-affine GF" is a hypothesis, not data. Build the
  avatar from the **top 20% of past workshop/training customers** (Hormozi's lost-
  chapter move; Chris Do concurs via "success leaves clues" + dinner-test), then cut
  it with the **camera-willingness filter** — which no backward analysis can supply
  (Chris Do); only the tripwire test itself delivers that datum. The quiz
  operationalizes it (Neil).
- *2Key (converged after cross-examination):* Chris Do's "2Key is the first sponsor
  in its own workshop" + Neil's "workshop participants ARE the avatar — a paying GF
  in the studio, not an n8n hobbyist" + Hormozi's clock: **compatible under one
  condition — the 90-day pre-order clock starts at memo signing.** With a date it's
  a test; without one it's the zombie Hormozi vetoes ("indecision is the heaviest
  burden"). If the test fails per R2 thresholds (≥50 paid pre-orders pass / <20
  fail), 2Key formally falls at the review and the sponsor slot goes to paying
  sponsors. Sales-team project: stays dead per R1. Neil's caveat stands: track it
  honestly (KPI activated accounts), don't run it as a fig leaf.
- *Scaling / exit / abroad-in-3-years:* Hormozi maps the route (2–5 trainers with
  mini-equity, license/franchise as the most sellable form, AI-avatar delivery;
  Alexander Storch candidate #1) but concedes: with the 3-year abroad plan this is
  realistically a **cash job with an expiry date — fine, if the cash flows into the
  asset**. Neil counters the timing: right map, wrong year — solve $1M problems at
  the $1M tier; AI augments, doesn't replace ("a German GF buying trust doesn't book
  an avatar"); Storch only after the tripwire passes, and two successful workshops
  from him before equity is even discussed. Chris Do deflects on exit (not his
  lane) but notes the recordings travel without Sebastian.
- *Cadence:* 1 workshop-video/week is the target; 3/week "is ego" (Chris Do) and
  breaks against the self-diagnosed consistency weakness (Neil). Shorts planned into
  the recording day upfront, not scavenged afterwards (Hormozi).
- *Comparison spiral (Chris Do, uncontested):* Julian-Ivanov envy is the inner
  critic plus a false-success narrative — "comparison is the thief of joy"; the only
  valid benchmark is your own trailing-12-month numbers. Operational: stop checking
  his channel; measure one-up-yourself monthly (Neil concurs).

**Dissent.**
- *Ads:* Chris Do holds "no ads — outbound instead: 5 dream companies per week,
  direct approach". Neil holds that small **retargeting** on video viewers + list
  after organic proof is cheap and belongs in the plan ("'no ads' as dogma is as
  wrong as 'ads immediately'"); Hormozi sides with Neil (ads only after organic
  proof). Outbound itself is unanimous. Founders: outbound starts now; retargeting
  decision at the October review.
- *Early checkpoint variant:* Hormozi wants **≥20 qualified first calls by day 30**
  (inputs short-term, outputs long-term); Chris Do wants **≥3 yeses by day 21**
  (three-yeses rule; he self-corrected his 3/60 threshold as too lax). Same
  function, different instrument — founders pick ONE at signing; the record refuses
  to average them.
- *Asset value of the format:* Hormozi holds "income play, period"; Chris Do holds
  the recordings are genuine productizable assets. Both agree the EXIT asset is
  2Key; the tension only governs how much energy the recordings' second life gets
  in year 1 (answer per sequencing: none until the tripwire passes).

**Assumptions.** (1) Companies pay full list price for an on-camera workshop —
UNTESTED; the tripwire exists precisely for this. (2) The Pierre recording is
representative of the format's energy/prep economics (n=1). (3) Hostinger will
formalize a quarterly commitment (if not, the sponsor leg of the flywheel is
hope, not revenue). (4) Florian's co-authorship of this direction is real and
survives the signing conversation (he was again not directly represented — third
council in a row; signature is the gate). (5) The studio can physically host 1–2
guests per session without breaking the YouTube production calendar.

**Rejected alternatives.**
- *"Workshops as THE business, 2Key on ice" (Sebastian's framing):* rejected
  unanimously — it re-decides the portfolio without a falsified assumption and
  converts 2Key into a focus-eating zombie; the flywheel fits INSIDE R2.
- *Premium-paywall long-form (YouTube Premium / paid access):* rejected 3:0 —
  wrong monetization floor; list-builder now, possibly a paid bundle later.
- *Advertising both delivery forms equally ("you choose: your site or ours"):*
  rejected — externally one thing; unadvertised tiers handle the rest.
- *Discount framing for the on-camera tier:* rejected after cross-examination
  (Hormozi and Neil retracted; binding no-discount wording rule).
- *Scaling structures now (trainer hires, license, AI-avatars):* rejected for
  year 1 (Neil's tier argument), kept as the post-tripwire map (Hormozi).
- *Killing 2Key outright today:* not chosen — the decided test with a started
  clock is cheaper than a kill without data and honors R2.
